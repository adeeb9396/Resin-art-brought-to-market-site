OUR FIELD PROJECT

Resin Art:
Brought to Market

Our field project explores how creative resin-art products can be introduced to customers through product presentation, market research, pricing, and digital marketing.

Our goal is to connect creativity with business opportunities and help people discover unique decorative products.

Our Objectives

  • Understand customer preferences.
  • Study product pricing and costs.
  • Explore digital marketing methods.
  • Present resin-art products online.
  • Identify future business opportunities.
